📖 Clinical Definitions
Glycemic Phenotype (GPF)
The category of glycemic pattern the patient falls into, based on TIR, hyperglycemia, and hypoglycemia thresholds.
Glycemic Burden Index (GBI)
Weighted severity score combining hyper- and hypoglycemia risk. Formula: (VH×3 + H×1 + Low×2 + VL×4) / 100. Higher = more dangerous.
Glycemic Polarity Map (GPM)
2D quadrant system mapping hyperglycemia vs. hypoglycemia load to determine the therapy direction: Intensify, Restructure, De-intensify, or Maintain.
